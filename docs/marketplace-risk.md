# Seller, authenticity, completeness and repost risk

Motivation uses transparent phrases such as moving/need gone/OBO and observed price-drop counts. It reports weak signals, explanation and confidence without inferring sensitive personal traits. Advertised original price is not a verified historical observation. Price history records each accepted observation; consecutive decreases generate deduplicated price-drop notifications.

Seller reliability considers supplied rating/review evidence and explicit red flags/scam phrases. Missing seller data returns unknown, not a bad score. These inputs can be misleading; the system neither verifies identities nor investigates people.

Authenticity outputs LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH/UNKNOWN with factors and a category checklist. Suspiciously low price and replica/scam wording can raise risk; they are not proof of counterfeiting. Unknown verification remains unknown. A user-entered verified flag is attributed input, not independent authentication. High risk blocks strong recommendations and advisory buy quotes. Personally inspect model/serial details, condition, provenance and category-specific identifiers before making a purchase.

Completeness distinguishes explicitly present, missing and unknown accessories. Only known missing items contribute illustrative replacement reserves. Gaming controller/power/HDMI, laptop charger and camera battery/charger/cap have transparent default reserves, not externally sourced quotes. Unknown accessories remain a warning rather than being silently assumed present or charged twice.

Duplicate matching uses similar titles plus specific seller/location/description/image evidence. Same model and price alone are insufficient. Heuristic match strengths are not calibrated probabilities. Linked reposts inherit earliest-known date and do not emit another new-opportunity event. Source IDs remain distinct and auditable.

No AI, vision, account access, private location inference, seller messaging, negotiation, payment or purchasing is performed. Exact arithmetic and matching remain deterministic.
